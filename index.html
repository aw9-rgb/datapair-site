Questions

Why are AI labs paying for business data?

AI has already learned from the public internet. To do real work, it now needs real examples of how businesses operate: email threads, support tickets, quotes, invoices and project files.

What kind of data sells?

Records your business created in the tools it already uses, such as email, chat, CRM, accounting, support and project software. More years of history and more systems usually mean a higher price.

How much could my data be worth?

Most published buyer payouts fall between $100K and $2M, depending on your industry, company size and the data you have. We'll tell you what we think yours is worth after your intake.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. We earn 10% only when a buyer pays you.

Do I have to send you my data?

No. Quotes are based on your intake answers alone. If you accept a deal, the data goes directly to the buyer and is anonymized before use. We never ask for passwords or customer records.

Will buyers know who I am?

Not unless you say so. Buyers see an anonymous summary until you choose to move forward.

What am I agreeing to?

A short agreement that lets us represent your data for 6 months. If we haven't brought you a quote within 60 days, you can walk away. You approve every introduction and sign every deal yourself.

Am I selling my business?

No. You're licensing a copy of your data. You keep your company, your customers and your operations.

How it works

You keep running your business. We do the matching, the paperwork and the back-and-forth with buyers.

  1. Tell us what you have

    Fill out the short form below. No files yet, just a description.

  2. We find interested buyers

    We shop a summary of your data to AI labs, without your name attached.

  3. Review and prepare

    We walk you through the offer. If needed, personal details are removed before anything is shared.

  4. Close and get paid

    You approve the final terms. Nothing is sold without your sign-off.

Data that sells

  • Records your business created through its own operations
  • Several years of history, or a large volume of entries
  • Structured exports, documents, emails, call notes or transcripts
  • Specialized fields: travel, logistics, real estate, healthcare billing, legal, trades

What we can't take

  • Data you don't own or don't have rights to sell
  • Personal information that can't be lawfully shared or de-identified
  • Data obtained by scraping or from another company's systems
  • Anything under a contract that prohibits resale
